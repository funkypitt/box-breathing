4 Minutes Breathing is a calm, distraction-free breathing app. Pick a shape, tap the button, and follow the dot round it for four minutes.

Each shape is drawn to its technique: its edges are as long as the breaths they stand for, so the dot moves at one steady pace and the distance left to go shows the time left to breathe.

- Square: box breathing 4-4-4-4, opening at 3 seconds and slowing to 4 halfway
- Triangle: 4-4-4, breathe in, hold, breathe out
- Long triangle: Dr Weil's 4-7-8 relaxing breath
- Petal: 3-3-6, an exhale twice as long as the inhale
- Circle: coherent breathing, 5-5, six breaths a minute

Every session lasts four minutes and ends on a complete breath. Bilingual prompts (English / French), the screen stays awake, no account, no ads, no tracking, no internet required.
