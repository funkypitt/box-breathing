# Privacy policy — 4 Minutes Breathing

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

4 Minutes Breathing (`com.freedomfighter.boxbreathing`) is published by Pierre Gallaz. It guides a four-minute breathing exercise by moving a dot around a shape. It is free, has no ads, no account and no tracking.

## Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library.

## Network use

None. The app never contacts a server: the shapes, the prompts and the sounds are all inside the app, and it works entirely offline. (The Android package lists the generic `INTERNET` permission because the framework it is built with, Expo / React Native, declares it; the app makes no use of it.)

## Data kept on the phone

One setting — whether the sound at each phase is on — is kept in the app's private storage on the phone. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled. Android's own backup may copy that setting to the Google account configured on the phone; that is controlled by the phone's backup settings, not by the app.

## Permissions

The app asks for nothing that needs your approval. It keeps the screen awake during the session and plays short sounds through the phone's speaker when you tick the sound option. It does not use the microphone, the camera, your location, your contacts or your files.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
